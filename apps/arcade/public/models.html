What loaded

Deep-link any model with ?src= — e.g. /models.html?src=/assets/crown/chars/kestrel/mesh-arms-clear.glb. The list is generated by scripts/model-catalogue.mjs; re-run it after adding models. three.js is vendored under /vendor/three, so this page has no runtime dependency on a CDN. Load a model with a skeleton and a Skeleton panel appears: sweep a joint through its range to see where a limb actually bends, nudge joints that sit in the wrong place, and export the corrections as JSON for tools/rigging to apply on the next rig.
