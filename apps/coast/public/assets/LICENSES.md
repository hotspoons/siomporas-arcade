# Third-party assets

All files under this directory are **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)** —
no attribution required, no restrictions. Kept here so the game can be split
out of the workspace with its assets.

| Folder | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `textures/asphalt.jpg` | ambientCG — Asphalt010 (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Asphalt010) | CC0 |

The Kenney kits are gone: every vehicle, tree, building and sign in the game is
now generated by `tools/assetgen` and lives in `generated/`. `draco/` is the
decoder those meshes need and `atlas/` is the sprite sheet baked from them.
