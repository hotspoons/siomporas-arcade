# CONCRETE CROWN — the twelve

*Twelve fighters, one crown.* An unsanctioned circuit that runs in the places a city forgot it
owned: a drained reservoir, a container yard, the roof of a car park, the floor of a foundry still
warm from the pour. No federation, no weight classes, no doctor. Whoever is standing at the end of
the season wears it.

---

## How these were built, and why they are safe to generate

Every one of these is an **archetype you already know wearing a person nobody has drawn**. That
split is deliberate and it is the whole legal argument.

What copyright and trade dress actually protect is **expression**: a specific character's name,
face, costume, colour scheme, catchphrase, logo, and the distinctive combination that makes them
recognisable. What they do not protect is **the idea**: a game with a fireball-and-uppercut
all-rounder, a slow grappler with a spinning command throw, a long-limbed zoner, a masked luchador.
Those are genre furniture, the way a first-person shooter has a shotgun. Move *inputs* —
quarter-circle-forward, charge back then forward — are functional method, not expression, and are
used across the entire genre by every publisher in it.

So the rules this roster follows, which the prompts in [ART.md](ART.md) enforce:

- **Never name a real game, studio or character in a prompt.** Not as a style reference, not as
  "in the style of", not "like the one from". It is the single fastest way to get output that is
  someone else's character with a hat on — and a prompt log that reads badly if anyone ever asks.
  Name the **era and the printing technique** instead. You get better art *and* clean provenance.
- **One recognisable silhouette per fighter, and it is theirs.** A bound club forearm, a mining
  winch for an arm, a hot stick, a drum at the hip. Not a headband, not a red gi.
- **No borrowed palette pairs.** Where an archetype has a famous costume colour, this roster goes
  somewhere else on purpose.
- **Nobody is a tribute.** If you can finish the sentence "he's basically ___", change him.

Two things still worth doing yourself before any of this goes on a marquee: search the name
`CONCRETE CROWN` and each fighter's name for a live game or trademark in your territory, and keep
the generated art and its prompts — provenance is the cheap part to have and the expensive part to
reconstruct. Also check the terms of whichever generator you use for what it says about commercial
use of its output; that varies by service and changes, and it is a separate question from
copyright.

I am not a lawyer and this is not advice — it is how the roster was designed to stay out of the way
of the question.

## Reading a fighter

**Silhouette** is the important line. At sprite size you have maybe 200 pixels of height and no
colour you can rely on, and the player has to know who just jumped at them from the shape alone.
Everything else is decoration.

**Role** is the archetype slot — what the character is *for*, in a roster that has to cover the
spread. **Specials** are the two motion-input moves; notation is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

**Prompt seed** is the paragraph you paste into the generator. It carries the body, the costume and
the silhouette, and nothing else — the style line and the layout instructions come from ART.md and
are the same for all twelve.

## 1. KESTREL — Wren Adisa

The one you learn the game on. Nigerian-British, out of a gym above a tyre fitter in Peckham.

- **Discipline** Dambe boxing, the bound-fist style, crossed with modern kickboxing.
- **Build** Tall, lean, very long limbs. Light on the feet.
- **Silhouette** The right forearm is bound wrist to elbow in heavy rope and cloth, thick as a club
  and unmistakable in profile. The left hand is bare. One arm is twice the other.
- **Palette** Rust-orange wrap, dark teal vest, bare feet, brass rings on the left hand.
- **Specials** *Kite Line* — a compressed-air punch that leaves a visible ring travelling forward.
  *Updraft* — a rising elbow into a knee, invincible on the way up.
- **Role** All-rounder, the tutorial character. Fireball, uppercut, honest walk speed.
- **Prompt seed** *A tall, lean Black British woman in her late twenties, shaved head, long limbs, a
  loose dark teal sleeveless vest and cropped rust-orange trousers, barefoot. Her right forearm is
  wrapped wrist to elbow in thick rust-orange rope and cloth so it reads as a club, twice the
  thickness of her bare left arm, which wears brass rings. Confident, relaxed, economical.*

## 2. BOLLARD — Duncan Mear

Forty years on the door of a Glasgow club that has changed its name six times.

- **Discipline** Scottish backhold wrestling, and doorway pragmatism.
- **Build** Enormous. Barrel chest, short legs, low centre of gravity. Reads as furniture.
- **Silhouette** A wardrobe with a head on it. Widest fighter in the game by a distance, and the
  only one whose outline is wider than it is tall in most poses.
- **Palette** A high-vis yellow-green vest gone grey with age, a red-and-black tartan sash, steel
  toecaps worn through to the metal.
- **Specials** *Bear Yoke* — a 360 command grab, the single most damaging move in the game.
  *Bollard Drop* — a running shoulder that ends with him planted and immovable.
- **Role** Grappler. Slow, no projectile, wins the moment he touches you.
- **Prompt seed** *An enormous white Scottish man in his late fifties, bald on top with grey stubble
  and a broken nose, barrel-chested with short thick legs and a low, planted stance. A filthy
  high-visibility yellow-green work vest gone grey, a red and black tartan sash across the chest,
  heavy steel-toecap boots worn through to bare metal. Calm, bored, immovable.*

## 3. SABLE PRIOR — Xun Meilin

Ring name kept because the real one is on a Vancouver court docket.

- **Discipline** Wing Chun, with a pair of short rattan escrima sticks.
- **Build** Small, compact, coiled. The shortest fighter on the roster.
- **Silhouette** Two short sticks, one per hand, held at forearm angles that never match. Reads as
  a fighter with four forearms.
- **Palette** Ink black quilted jacket, jade green cuffs and collar, black canvas shoes.
- **Specials** *Chain Punch* — a rapid-fire straight-line string, mashable, the fastest startup in
  the game. *Split Bamboo* — charge back, then forward: a stick thrust that goes through
  projectiles.
- **Role** Rushdown. Gets in, stays in, dies quickly if she has to come back in.
- **Prompt seed** *A short, compact Chinese-Canadian woman in her early thirties, black hair cut
  blunt at the jaw, wiry and coiled. A black quilted short jacket with jade green cuffs and collar,
  black trousers, flat black canvas shoes. She holds a short rattan stick in each hand at different
  angles, close to the body. Watchful, still, about to move.*

## 4. KOMBINAT — Yeva Ostrenko

Was a rigger on a Donbas mine head. The arm is her own work.

- **Discipline** Sambo, plus whatever the arm can be persuaded to do.
- **Build** Tall, heavy through the shoulders, thick-waisted. Solid rather than muscular.
- **Silhouette** The left arm below the elbow is a mining winch — a hook and a drum of cable, longer
  and squarer than a limb should be. The cable pays out behind her when she moves.
- **Palette** Gunmetal and coal dust, safety red on the winch housing, a grey singlet.
- **Specials** *Winch* — half-circle forward: the hook fires full screen, and on a hit **she is
  pulled to them**, not the reverse. It closes distance rather than stealing a knockdown, which is
  what keeps it from being everyone else's grab. *Flywheel* — the arm spins and eats projectiles.
- **Role** Grappler who can start from full screen. Terrifying at range, mediocre up close.
- **Prompt seed** *A tall, solidly built Ukrainian woman in her forties, ash-blonde hair scraped
  back, coal dust worked into the lines of her face and neck. Grey singlet, heavy canvas work
  trousers. Her left arm below the elbow is a salvaged mining winch: a squared steel housing painted
  safety red, a drum of steel cable, and a heavy hook where the hand should be, longer and heavier
  than her right arm. Loose cable trails behind her. Unbothered.*

## 5. FATHOM — Tui Reweti-Kane

Deep-sea salvage out of Auckland. Comes up when the money is good.

- **Discipline** Mau rākau, the Māori staff art, on a freediver's conditioning.
- **Build** Heavyset but fluid — thick through the middle, and quick with it.
- **Silhouette** A staff longer than he is tall, weighted at one end, held across the body. His
  outline is mostly a diagonal line.
- **Palette** Deep ocean blue, bone white, oxidised copper. Long hair tied high.
- **Specials** *Undertow* — a low staff sweep that drags the opponent toward him. *Breath Hold* —
  hold the button: armour for as long as he holds it, and his own damage climbs while he does.
- **Role** The long poke. Controls space with normals rather than projectiles.
- **Prompt seed** *A heavyset Māori-Samoan man in his mid thirties, long black hair tied high, a
  full sleeve of traditional tattoo on one arm and shoulder. Bare-chested, deep ocean blue work
  trousers, a wide braided belt, bare feet. He carries a two-metre hardwood staff weighted with
  oxidised copper at one end, held diagonally across his body. Grounded, patient, heavy.*

## 6. CANDELA — Rosalía Mbeki-Ferrán

Equatoguinean-Spanish. Danced professionally until the knee went, which it did not.

- **Discipline** Capoeira, with flamenco footwork bolted on.
- **Build** Athletic, mid-height, never still. Always mid-transition.
- **Silhouette** Inverted more often than not — hands on the floor, feet in the air. The only
  fighter whose head is regularly below her knees.
- **Palette** Sun yellow, blood orange, bare midriff, ankle bells, wrapped hands.
- **Specials** *Ember Wheel* — a rolling kick arc that crosses up. *Zapateado* — a stomp string
  that lays a fire trail on the floor and keeps burning after she has moved on.
- **Role** Mixup. Nothing she does is where it looks like it is.
- **Prompt seed** *An athletic Equatoguinean-Spanish woman in her late twenties, dark curls tied
  back with a yellow cloth, mid-motion and off balance on purpose. A cropped sun-yellow top, loose
  blood-orange trousers cut off below the knee, bare midriff, hands wrapped, small bells at both
  ankles. Fluid, grinning, about to invert.*

## 7. MERIDIAN — Dr Anouk Feraud

Sports scientist, struck off. Tests the brace on herself because nobody else will sign for it.

- **Discipline** Savate, through a powered leg brace of her own design.
- **Build** Lean, precise, technical. Kicks land where she says they will.
- **Silhouette** Both legs are exposed actuator frames from hip to ankle — visible mechanism, not
  armour. Her legs read as machinery and her top half does not.
- **Palette** Lab white, cyan telemetry lines, oxblood savate boots.
- **Specials** *Chassé Arc* — charge down, then up: a rising kick with a cyan arc behind it.
  *Overclock* — spends meter for speed, and the brace runs hot: while it is on, she takes more.
- **Role** Charge character. High execution, high reward, punishes anyone who guesses.
- **Prompt seed** *A lean French-Algerian woman in her late thirties, dark hair in a severe short
  cut, clinical and composed. A fitted white technical top with fine cyan line-work. From hip to
  ankle both legs are enclosed in an exposed powered brace — visible actuators, carbon struts,
  cable runs, glowing cyan telemetry seams — clearly mechanism rather than armour. Oxblood leather
  boots. Balanced on one leg, the other cocked.*

## 8. THRESHER — Gus Vanterpool

Offshore roughneck until the rig went. Fights for cash in a drained reservoir.

- **Discipline** None whatsoever.
- **Build** Heavy, gone to seed over enormous underlying strength. Gut, arms, scars.
- **Silhouette** A length of anchor chain runs from his right fist to his belt and swings behind
  every move he makes. The only trailing element on the roster.
- **Palette** Oil black, sun-bleached denim, rust, a lot of exposed sunburnt skin.
- **Specials** *Chain Whip* — the longest horizontal reach in the game, and slow enough to be
  jumped. *Gut Check* — an armoured headbutt that walks through one hit to land.
- **Role** The ugly one. Slow, mean, frightening, and losing badly is part of his design.
- **Prompt seed** *A big, run-down white American man in his fifties, sunburnt and scarred, thinning
  hair, a heavy gut over obvious old strength. Bare-chested under an open oil-stained denim vest,
  faded jeans, work boots. A length of rusted anchor chain is wrapped round his right fist and runs
  down to his belt, hanging loose. Bad-tempered, unhurried, filthy.*

> Time Killers-flavoured gore is a **toggle**, off by default, and it is a shader and a particle
> emitter rather than an art requirement. Do not generate dismemberment frames. See DESIGN.md.

## 9. OSSUARY — name unknown

Mexico City. Has never been seen without the mask, including by his family, who are fine with it.

- **Discipline** Lucha libre, técnico, high-flying.
- **Build** Classic V-taper. The most conventionally heroic body on the roster, on purpose.
- **Silhouette** The mask's bone crest — a vertical fin running front to back over the skull.
  Recognisable from behind, which nothing else on the roster is.
- **Palette** Bone white, cardinal red, gold trim. Cape only on the victory pose.
- **Specials** *Tope Suicida* — a diving headbutt that travels most of the screen. *Reliquary* — a
  multi-hit air grab, the only command throw in the game that works on an airborne opponent.
- **Role** Aerial grappler. Owns the space above the stage.
- **Prompt seed** *A powerfully built Mexican luchador in his thirties, classic V-tapered build. A
  full-head bone-white lucha mask with a raised vertical bone-ridge crest running front to back over
  the skull, cardinal red eye and mouth trim, gold filigree. Cardinal red trunks with gold detailing,
  white boots laced to the knee, bone-white wrist wraps. Bare-chested. Theatrical, arms wide.*

## 10. VESSEL — Sunwoo Ha-eun

Seventeen. Lied on the entry form. Her grandmother's drum.

- **Discipline** Taekkyeon, timed to pungmul drumming.
- **Build** Slight, wiry, fast. Youngest and lightest on the roster.
- **Silhouette** A janggu — an hourglass drum — slung at her left hip, and a light stick in her
  right hand she plays it with between exchanges.
- **Palette** Pale hanbok blue, vermillion sash, white socks, bare arms.
- **Specials** *Pungmul* — a drum strike that sends a shockwave along the floor. *Pumbaek* — the
  taekkyeon push-kick, which does almost no damage and enormous pushback.
- **Role** The gimmick. She has a **beat**: a metronome runs under her, and moves landed on it do
  meaningfully more. Learn the tempo, get a different character.
- **Prompt seed** *A slight Korean girl of seventeen, wiry and fast, black hair in a high ponytail
  tied with vermillion cloth. A pale blue sleeveless hanbok-style top over loose white trousers
  gathered at the ankle, a vermillion sash, white split-toe socks. An hourglass-shaped janggu drum
  is slung at her left hip on a strap, and she holds a slim bamboo beater in her right hand. Light
  on her feet, chin up, unimpressed.*

## 11. GLASSJAW BILL — William Okonkwo-Hart

Sixty-one. Should not be here. Keeps winning, which is the problem.

- **Discipline** Bare-knuckle boxing. Four decades of it.
- **Build** Lean, old, immaculate posture. Visibly the oldest and visibly the most dangerous.
- **Silhouette** A tweed waistcoat over a bare torso, and hand wraps. No shirt, no gloves. The only
  fighter dressed like he came from somewhere else.
- **Palette** Bone, dust brown, tobacco. Blood on the wraps from round one onward.
- **Specials** *Counter Book* — a parry that gets stronger the more times the opponent has used
  the move he parries. *Dead Eye* — a one-frame counterpunch off the parry, and the highest single
  hit in the game.
- **Role** Execution check. Lowest health on the roster, and he only needs to be right twice.
- **Prompt seed** *A lean Australian man of sixty-one with dark skin and close-cropped white hair,
  a scarred face and an immaculate upright stance. Bare-chested under an open brown tweed waistcoat,
  high-waisted dust-brown trousers with braces hanging loose at the hips, bare feet. Both hands are
  wrapped in dirty bone-white cloth, marked with blood. Utterly calm, guard low, chin tucked.*

## 12. ARCLIGHT — Sana Bhattarai

High-altitude linesman. Works the towers over the Kathmandu valley in weather that closes airports.

- **Discipline** Kalaripayattu, with a lineman's insulated hot stick.
- **Build** Tall, sinewy, wire-thin. Longest reach on the roster after Fathom.
- **Silhouette** A two-metre insulated hot stick with a hooked fitting at the end, and a harness of
  coiled orange cable across her back that never quite settles.
- **Palette** Ultraviolet purple, electrical-tape black, hazard orange, a dull grey harness.
- **Specials** *Earth Fault* — charge back then forward: an arc that travels along the floor and
  cannot be jumped over cleanly. *Grounding* — absorbs an incoming projectile and converts it to
  meter.
- **Role** Zoner. Wins from a screen away and has almost nothing up close.
- **Prompt seed** *A tall, wire-thin Nepali woman in her thirties, weather-beaten, black hair in a
  practical braid. A dull grey climbing harness over a deep ultraviolet-purple technical jacket with
  black electrical tape wrapped at the forearms and shins, hazard-orange gloves, heavy boots. Coils
  of orange cable are slung across her back. She holds a two-metre insulated fibreglass hot stick
  with a hooked metal fitting at the end. Squinting, braced against wind.*

## Order of work

If you are not generating all twelve at once — and you should not be, the first two will teach you
what to change about the prompts — do them in this order:

1. **Kestrel** and **Bollard**. Fast versus slow, small versus enormous. If those two read as
   different fighters at sprite size, the pipeline is working.
2. **Ossuary** and **Arclight**. A conventional body and a very unconventional one.
3. The rest in any order. **Kombinat**'s winch arm and **Meridian**'s leg brace are the two most
   likely to need several attempts; leave them until the prompts have settled.
